How it works

Print the template

Three A4 pages with a box for every character.

Fill it in by hand

One character per box, in your own writing. Takes about twenty minutes.

Upload the three pages

Scan or photograph them. We check each page is the right one, the right way up.

Type with your handwriting

Preview it, try some formulas, and download the .ttf.

What it covers

191 characters: the Latin alphabet in both cases, digits, punctuation, the full Greek alphabet, and the mathematical, scientific and chemical symbols needed for real notation — ∫ ∑ ∏ ∂ ∇ √ ± ≠ ≤ ≥ ⇌ ∈ ∞ and more.

Superscripts and subscripts reuse your own glyphs rather than asking you to write them twice, so x² is your handwritten 2, made smaller and lifted.
