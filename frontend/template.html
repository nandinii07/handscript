Four pages, 128 characters. Every box carries a printed guide — baseline, x-height, cap-height, ascender and descender — so you align to a shared grid while still writing in your own hand. This is the only part that takes any real time.

Printing

  • Print all four pages. Page 1 is uppercase and lowercase letters, page 2 is numbers, punctuation and brackets, page 3 is operators, math symbols, Greek and superscripts.
  • Page 4 is reference only — it explains what each guide line means. There is nothing to write on it, and you will not upload it.
  • Print at 100% scale — not "fit to page". Scaling shrinks the boxes and leaves you less room to write.
  • Plain white A4 or Letter, one page per sheet.

Writing

  • Use a dark pen with a reasonably thick stroke. Pencil and very light ink can be too faint to pick up.
  • Keep the same pen and the same writing style on every page, and keep the same slant throughout — the guides only correct position and scale, not shape.
  • Rest normal letters on the solid blue baseline. Lowercase bodies reach the x-height line, uppercase reaches the cap-height line, and ascenders/descenders (b d f h k l t / g j p q y) reach their own guide.
  • Write inside each box and don't touch the printed border — ink crossing a border can confuse the box detection.
  • The small character printed under each box tells you what to write in it. Do not skip any box — a skipped box becomes a blank character in your font.
  • Leave any box with a faint grey X inside it empty. Those are unused slots, printed as boxes so the grid stays regular, not characters to write.
  • Punctuation follows its own printed guide rather than sitting centred — the period sits on the baseline, the comma's tail drops below it.

Scanning

  • A scanner at 300 DPI is best. A phone works if you use a scanning app such as Adobe Scan or the Notes app, which straighten the page for you.
  • Keep the page flat and the whole page in frame, including all four corner registration marks and the heading at the top.
  • Do not crop the edges or the heading — the heading is how we detect an upside-down page.
  • Even lighting, no shadow across the sheet, no glare.
  • Upload pages 1, 2 and 3, in that order. Each has a different number of boxes, so we check you have the right page in the right slot before building anything. Page 4 is not uploaded.
